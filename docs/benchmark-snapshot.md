## Methodology

- Command: `make benchmark-artifacts`
- Clock: `time.process_time`; process CPU time, not wall-clock latency.
- Per case: 1 untimed warm-up invocation(s), then 7 measured batches; each batch repeats the operation count recorded per workload.
- The reported time is the median batch total. Payload construction and decoder/extractor setup are outside the timed batch.
